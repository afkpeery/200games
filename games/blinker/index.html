How to play

  1. Start the round and press keys as fast as you can for one minute. Each separate press scores a point.
  2. A random bonus key flashes for 2 seconds. Match it to score 2×, 5×, or 7× points for the next 3 seconds.
  3. On a touchscreen, use Tap to score and tap the bonus key when it appears.
