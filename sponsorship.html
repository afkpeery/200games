Sponsor placements

The game directory and BLINKER each have one text placement. A booked placement displays the sponsor’s name, a “Sponsored” label and one link. An open placement displays its availability. All games stay free to play.

What we count

200 Games sends anonymous counters for page loads, first BLINKER starts, visible placements and sponsor link clicks. A placement qualifies after at least half of it is visible for one continuous second in a foreground tab. Each event counts at most once per page load. Open placements and paid sponsors have separate counters.

The counter receives a page label and an event type. It uses no tracking cookies or visitor IDs. A sponsor link includes campaign tags so the sponsor can identify incoming traffic. Test visits are marked separately. These totals measure events and cannot tell us how many distinct people played.

Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track turn measurement off. You can also save your choice on this browser.
